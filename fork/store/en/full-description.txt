BirdyGo listens to the birds around you and tells you who is singing, with no internet connection.

OFFLINE IDENTIFICATION
Tap Listen: the song analysis runs entirely on your phone, with the BirdNET+ model. No recording leaves the device. Handy in the forest, in the mountains, in the garden, and even with the screen off and the phone in your pocket.

CLEAR RESULTS
Each species is rated Sure, Probable or To check, so you know right away how far to trust it. The app uses place and season to set aside unlikely species. Results are estimates: you confirm or dismiss each detection.

YOUR OBSERVATION NOTEBOOK
Every listening session is kept with the species heard, the time, the place and an audio clip to replay. Find your birds by species, by day or by place.

QUIZ AND PROGRESS
Train your ear with the quiz, earn ranks and follow your daily goals. The game only rewards sure detections or ones you confirmed.

MAPS
The contacts map shows where each bird was heard (OpenStreetMap base maps, loaded only if you allow it). On every species page, a world map shows where the species lives and in which season, even offline.

SPECIES PAGES
Photo, description and range map for hundreds of species, available offline.

SOUND LIBRARY
Replay the clips you kept.

FOUR BIRDS TO CHOOSE FROM
Pick the app's companion: oriole, bee-eater, flamingo or starling, each with its own colours.

SHARE YOUR OBSERVATIONS
Export your sessions (CSV, GPX, JSON, HTML report). Nothing is sent unless you do it.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Audio and location stay on your phone. Maps, place names, weather and large photos only load if you turn them on in Settings > Privacy. For ages 13 and over.
