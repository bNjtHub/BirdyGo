regions_admin1.bin.gz: derived from Natural Earth 1:10m Cultural Vectors,
"Admin 1 - States, Provinces" (ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces). Natural Earth
data is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
The country borders in the same file are the dissolved admin-1 regions.

gadm1_to_regions.json.gz: a table of identifiers only (GADM level-1 ids, as
GBIF reports them, and Natural Earth admin-1 codes). No GADM geometry or
attribute is included. The table was made by asking the GBIF geocoding API
(api.gbif.org/v1/geocode/reverse) which GADM region lies at a few points of
each Natural Earth region.

Both built by tools/fork_world_regions.py (formats described in its header).
